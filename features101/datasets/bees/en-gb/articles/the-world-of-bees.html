The world of bees is a complex, highly organized and vital ecosystem, featuring over 20,000 known species that act as the primary pollinators for the planet's plants and food crops. These insects have evolved over 120 million years, diversifying into varied habitats worldwide, from arid deserts to the edges of the Arctic.

Social Bees (Honeybees and Bumblebees) live in highly organized colonies with a strict hierarchy: one queen, thousands of female workers and many male drones. Solitary Bees (Most Species) live alone: an individual builds a nest, often in the ground or in wood, and acts as both queen and worker for her offspring.
