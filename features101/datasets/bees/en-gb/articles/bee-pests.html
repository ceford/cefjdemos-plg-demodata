Bee colonies can be affected by a range of pests that weaken the insects or damage the hive. Some pests feed on bees, while others consume wax, pollen, or stored honey. A healthy colony is often able to resist small numbers of pests, but large infestations can reduce the colony's strength and make it more vulnerable to disease.

Beekeepers regularly inspect their hives for signs of pest activity. Good hive management, regular cleaning, and maintaining strong colonies help to reduce many problems. When pests are detected early, appropriate control measures can often prevent serious damage while minimising disruption to the bees and their environment.
