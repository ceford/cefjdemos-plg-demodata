Bee colonies may be affected by bacterial, fungal, viral, or parasitic diseases. Some illnesses spread quickly between bees, while others develop slowly and become noticeable only after the colony has weakened. Good nutrition and favourable environmental conditions help bees to remain healthy and better able to withstand disease.

Beekeepers monitor their colonies throughout the year for signs of poor health, such as unusual behaviour or declining numbers of bees. Early identification of disease allows appropriate action to be taken, helping to protect both managed honey bees and nearby wild pollinators.
